EA Assistant privacy policy

EA Assistant is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What it reads

With the owner's consent, it reads his own Gmail and Google Calendar, read-only (gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly). It never sends, changes or deletes mail or calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner keep track of his own commitments and schedule. It is kept on the owner's own computer, is not sold, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. Parts of it may be processed by an AI model service the owner uses, only to do this work for him.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Saved data is deleted from the owner's computer when it is no longer needed.
